Company privacy policy

Our original company Privacy Policy applies to Priceless Finance as an extension of our business. It explains collection, use, disclosure, storage, retention and individual rights. This notice supplements that policy for this website.

We process customer contact details, vehicle enquiries, offer details and information submitted in finance applications to respond to enquiries, administer offers and arrange requested finance. Information may be shared with relevant service providers, finance brokers and lenders as necessary for these purposes. Our company policy describes retention and your rights.

Google account and Gmail integration

Authorised members of staff may connect the company Google account to send personalised vehicle offer emails using the Gmail API. We request permission to send email on behalf of the connected account (gmail.send), and basic account identity information (OpenID and email) to verify the authorised account. We do not request permission to read, search or modify the Gmail inbox.

The integration uses the authorised account identity, OAuth credentials, recipient email address, offer details and message content to authenticate and send requested messages. Google receives the recipient and message content in order to deliver the email. OAuth refresh credentials are stored encrypted and access is restricted to authorised staff. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide the requested email functionality, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and data requests

The connected account owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Contact us to request removal of stored connection credentials or to exercise your data protection rights. For privacy enquiries and data access requests, email dsar@pricelessautoservices.com. Complaints may be sent to complaints@pricelessautoservices.com. You may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

Commission disclosure

If you are an individual, sole trader or small partnership and we are arranging a credit agreement or a consumer hire agreement for you which is regulated under the Consumer Credit Act 1974, you have a right to ask us to disclose the income that we will receive for arranging finance for you. If you make such a request, we will disclose the amount to you without delay. You can make a disclosure request at any time and by any means including over the phone, by email, in writing or at the dealership. You may, email pricelesscarsales1@gmail.com or write to Commission Disclosure Priceless Auto Services Ltd, Old Warrington Road, Northwich, England, CW9 5LH or if you are at the dealership, please speak with your Sales Executive/Sales Manager.
